Methodology

From public filings to tested hypotheses

The analysis is one pipeline. Select a step for its explanation.

1 · Public SEC filings

Filing lists from the EDGAR submissions API; the primary annual report of every fiscal year FY2004–2024; the proxy statement of every filing year; XBRL company facts; 8-K Items 4.01 and 4.02; every amended annual report.

2 · Filing and evidence extraction

Item 1, Item 9A, Item 13, the related-party note and the audit report are located by heading; the proxy's related-party section replaces Item 13 when it is incorporated by reference. Every coded value keeps the sentence it came from. Texts under 40 words are treated as missing.

3 · Eight related-party characteristics

Financial: lending to, payables to, sales to and purchases from related parties, scaled by assets or revenue. Disclosure: incompleteness (counterparty, amount, terms, purpose) and vagueness (Loughran–McDonald uncertainty and weak-modal words). Governance: board non-independence and CEO duality from the proxy statement.

4 · Rank-based score construction

Each characteristic is ranked 0–1 against the 1411 analysed firm-years (average ranks for ties); a layer score is the mean rank of the characteristics observed; the risk score is ten times the mean of the layer scores that exist. Nothing is imputed.

5 · Matched case/control comparison

Pr(case firm-year) = Λ(αset + β·layer + γ·leverage + δ·ROA), conditional on the matched set, so each case is compared only with its own matched firms. β is per standard deviation; standard errors are clustered by firm because a firm's five years are not independent.

6 · Five hypothesis tests

H1–H3: one layer each. H4: the same tests in each event year. H5: Model A (traditional index + controls) against Model B (plus the risk score), each matched set scored by a model fitted without it, and the first year each rule flags a case at an equal false-alarm rate.

7 · Multiple-testing correction

H1–H3 are three tests of one idea, so Holm's step-down adjustment is applied. Each hypothesis is directional, and since 17 October 2026 (researcher decision, recorded in the design amendment after the results were known) each is tested one-sided in the predicted direction; the two-sided values are reported beside them. H4 applies the same rule across its fifteen year-by-layer tests.

8 · Interpretation

Results are associations within matched sets, observed before public disclosure. Matched firms are firms without enforcement, not firms known to be clean.

Risk-score construction: the five steps

The 6 October design builds the AI risk score in five steps. Each block shows the step as the design states it, what the code does at that step, and what it produces on the 1411 analysed firm-years. Nothing in this score is learned; it is the pre-registered construction.

  1. 1Raw figures
  2. 2Eight characteristics
  3. 3Rank 0–1
  4. 4Layer scores
  5. 5AI risk score 0–10
1

Raw figures are entered for each firm-year

Design. Related-party amounts, the four disclosure items, word counts and board figures.

What the code does. The related-party coder reads the financial-statement note and the Item 13 or proxy section separately and keeps every amount with its sentence. For each flow it takes, in order, the note's fiscal-year total, the firm's XBRL related-party tag, then the Item 13 total. It enters zero when the firm reports no transaction of that type, and leaves the value missing when a transaction is described without an amount. The disclosure items are flagged from the text. Board size, independent directors and CEO duality come from the proxy statement.

3

Each characteristic is ranked against all firm-years, from 0 to 1

Design. Characteristics on one scale, not driven by a few extreme firms.

What the code does. Each characteristic is converted to its percentile rank among the 1411 analysed firm-years (pandas rank(pct=True), average ranks for ties). A missing value stays missing; nothing is imputed. One difference from the design's wording: the design says "each continuous characteristic", but the code also ranks CEO duality, which is 0/1, so every duality value takes one of two tied ranks.
