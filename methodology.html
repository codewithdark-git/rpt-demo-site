Methodology

Three stages, eighteen blocks. The diagram is the method; the notes under it say what each block does. The written chapter, with every formula and rule, is here.

Stage 1 · Sample and data

  1. Case discoveryEvery SEC accounting release 2005–2024, every litigation release 2010–2025, EDGAR full-text search, the client workbook.
  2. Inclusion D1The order must describe a misstatement or misappropriation through an RPT, or a charge for not disclosing one.
  3. Revelation date D2The first public event that revealed the problem, with its source and a confidence level.
  4. Window D3The five fiscal years before the revelation year; at least four annual reports.
  5. Matching D4Five firms from the same industry, nearest in size, with a 10-K every window year and no enforcement.
  6. DownloadFiling lists, 10-Ks, proxies, XBRL values, 8-K items and 10-K/As from the SEC's public endpoints.

Stage 2 · Measurement

  1. ExtractionItem 1, Item 9A, Item 13, the related-party note, the audit report, the proxy's related-party section.
  2. Eight characteristicsLending, payables, sales, purchases; incompleteness, vagueness; board non-independence, CEO duality.
  3. Traditional signalsMaterial weakness, modified or going-concern opinion, auditor change, restatement.
  4. ControlsLeverage and return on assets; industry, size and year through the matching.
  5. ScoresRank each characteristic 0–1, average within layer, average the layers, times ten.

Stage 3 · Tests and validation

  1. M1–M3Coverage, reliability, distinctness.
  2. H1–H3Each layer against fraud within matched sets; Holm-adjusted; firm-clustered errors.
  3. H4The same test in each of the five years.
  4. H5Traditional signals alone against traditional plus the score, out of set; timing at equal false-alarm rate.
  5. P1Transaction types by form of fraud.
  6. A1–A3Score divided into named evidence; stability when each set is left out; reading against theory.
  7. ValidationAlternative samples, external set, power, deviations log; the fitted model kept apart as exploratory.

How a firm-year is scored

rank each characteristic against the 862 window firm-years → layer score = mean rank of the observed characteristics → AI risk score = 10 × mean of the layer scores → flagged if above the matched firms' 95th percentile
