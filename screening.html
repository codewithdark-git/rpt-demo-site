From candidates to cases

Every candidate was screened by the four rules: an RPT at the centre of the charge (D1), a dated first public revelation (D2), at least four annual reports in the five years before it (D3), and matched firms (D4).

Confidence in the revelation date

High: a dated SEC filing or order. Medium: a documented public event. Low: the SEC action date stands in. A corrected date re-labels the window years; the filings are already on file.

How close the matched firms are

Gap in log total assets between each matched firm and its case in the year before revelation, and the industry level at which the match was found. Three sets are poorly size-matched because their industries have few firms of the case's size.
