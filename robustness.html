Statistical robustness

What changes the answer, and what does not

Every alternative shown here was run on the same data by the same code. Nothing below is hypothetical. Tables for each chart are in the workbook; the review that prompted most of these checks is here.

Firm-clustered errors double the intervals

Why. A firm's five window years are not five independent observations: duality and the stated amounts barely change within a firm. The design asks for errors clustered by firm. Effect. Governance loses significance; the financial and disclosure results were already not significant.

Holm adjustment across H1–H3

Why. Three tests of one idea raise the chance of a false positive. Effect. The adjustment moves the model-based p-values but changes no conclusion on its own.

The sample moves precision, not direction

What was varied. 21 misstatement/misappropriation cases only; 30 cases before Gulfport was approved; the 31-case main sample; 36 cases with five three-report cases (relaxing the four-report rule); and ranking against every FY2004–2024 firm-year instead of the window only. Effect. Adding the disclosure-only cases halves the governance odds ratio; nothing changes sign.

Thresholds and the H2 re-runs

Thresholds. The AI rule is compared with the traditional rule at the 5 % matched-firm rate and at the traditional rule's own, lower rate; the conclusion is the same. H2 re-runs. Without the disclosure-only cases (the design's second run), without texts that state "no transactions", and each characteristic alone.

Missing data and sample restrictions

Rule. A characteristic that is not stated stays missing; a layer score is the mean of the characteristics observed; a firm-year with no observed characteristic has no score. No imputation in the design tests. Effect. The financial layer is observed in about a fifth of firm-years and more often in case firms, so H1 is tested on a subset selected on the outcome.

Stability of the attribution and power of the test

A2. Re-ranking without each matched set in turn changes the largest contributing layer in 1.0 % of firm-years. Power. Simulated power of the within-set test with firm-clustered errors; the layer tests use 16–25 informative sets, so their power is nearer the 21-set bars.
