Coverage

Amounts of related-party transactions are stated in about a quarter of fraud firm-years and a tenth of matched firm-years. The financial layer is therefore tested on a subset selected on the outcome.

Distinctness

The three layer scores are nearly uncorrelated. Each layer carries its own information.

H1–H3: the three layers within matched sets

Odds of being a fraud firm-year per one standard deviation of the layer score, each case against its own matched firms. Governance points the predicted way; the financial layer only on the disclosing subset; the disclosure layer points the other way. The firm-clustered intervals are the design's test.

H2 re-runs and single characteristics
