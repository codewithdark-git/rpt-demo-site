Companies used in the research

This document lists every firm in the study: the 31 qualifying RPT-fraud cases of the main sample (21 misstatement/misappropriation, 10 disclosure-only including Gulfport Energy, approved on 10 October 2026; all FRAUD = 1 in the design tests) with their rule-matched firms, the 10 external-validation cases with theirs, and the five pilot anchors with the hand-picked competitors used in the pilot. Facts are taken from the project's own files: the cases files, the Phase A screen (scripts/phase_a_screen_v2.csv), the candidate notes (scripts/candidates_v02.csv), the matching tables (scripts/peers_matched_*.csv), the EDGAR company tables of the builds, and the run-3 model outputs. Matching rule D4: same four-digit SIC code (fallback three-, then two-digit), nearest log total assets in the fiscal year before revelation, a 10-K on file in every window year, and no SEC accounting enforcement match. 'Gap in ln(assets)' is the absolute difference between the matched firm's and the case's log total assets at the anchor year (0.69 = a factor of two).
