Chapter 4 — Experimental set-up and results

4.1 Experimental set-up

Element Setting
Main sample 31 RPT-fraud cases (21 misstatement/misappropriation, 10 disclosure-only including Gulfport Energy; all FRAUD = 1) and 145 matched firms in 31 sets; 862 window firm-years, 152 of them fraud firm-years (17.6%)
Alternative samples (§4.9) 21 misstatement/misappropriation cases only; 30 (before Gulfport); 36 (+ five three-report cases, D3 relaxed, for information); 31 with ranks over all FY2004–2024 firm-years
Primary tests conditional logit within matched sets with firm-clustered errors (H1–H4); leave-one-set-out logit AUC with set bootstrap (H5); direct arithmetic (A1–A3); 300-replication simulation (power)
Exploratory model L2 logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, mean-probability ensemble; leave-one-matched-set-out (21 folds on the core sample); nested 5-fold text stacking; 2,000 cluster-bootstrap resamples; threshold 0.702 (matched-firm rate ≤ 5 %)
External set 10 three-report cases, 28 matched firms, 167 window firm-years, scored once with the exploratory model
Data data_core (176 firms, 2,669 annual reports, FY2004–2024, including the merged Gulfport set), data_sens (38 firms); every 10-K/A of every firm read for restatement language
Hardware and time 4-core workstation; core data build 3.5 h with two SEC workers; design battery about 10 minutes per sample; exploratory model about 3 h per configuration

4.1.1 Development stage (pilot)

Before the confirmatory sample existed the pipeline was developed on five pilot anchors (Ault Alliance, ProPetro, Co-Diagnostics, Gulfport, Skechers) with hand-picked competitors. On the FY2004–2024 pilot build (24 firms, 4 usable sets, 107 window firm-years) the tree models reached out-of-fold AUC 0.68 with intervals spanning 0.36–0.95; the pilot established that the pipeline ran leak-free on real filings and that related-party amounts are rarely tagged in XBRL. It produced no thesis result.

Table 4.8 — H1–H3, conditional logit within matched sets (odds ratio per SD; model-based and firm-clustered; Holm across the three)

hypothesis layer_score n (firm-years / firms / sets) OR per SD [95 % CI] p p Holm firm-clustered 95 % CI p firm-cl. p Holm firm-cl. direction supported (model-based) supported (firm-clustered)
H1 L1_SCORE 187 / 63 / 17 1.61 [1.04, 2.49] 0.031 0.062 [0.77, 3.38] 0.206 0.459 as predicted no no
H2 L2_SCORE 517 / 117 / 25 0.80 [0.63, 1.02] 0.075 0.075 [0.49, 1.31] 0.374 0.459 opposite no no
H3 L3_SCORE 284 / 71 / 18 1.70 [1.22, 2.37] 0.002 0.006 [0.82, 3.51] 0.153 0.459 as predicted yes no

H1. The financial layer is higher in fraud firm-years (OR 1.61 per SD, 95 % CI 1.04–2.49) on the 187 firm-years of 63 firms in 17 sets that state an amount; p = 0.031 unadjusted, 0.062 Holm, 0.206 firm-clustered. By the design rule H1 is not supported with firm-clustered errors, and the subset is selected on the outcome (Table 4.2). H2. The disclosure layer runs the opposite way: fraud firms' related-party text is more complete and plainer than their matched firms' (OR 0.80, 95 % CI 0.63–1.02; p = 0.075, Holm = 0.075, firm-clustered = 0.374). H2 is not supported. H3. The governance layer is higher in fraud firm-years (OR 1.70, 95 % CI 1.22–2.37; p 0.002, Holm = 0.006; firm-clustered p = 0.153, Holm = 0.459). H3 is not supported with the firm-clustered errors the design asks for and supported with model-based errors. The layer is carried by CEO duality: board share is observed in 15 % of firm-years.

The firm-clustered errors are about twice the model-based ones because the layer scores change little within a firm across the five window years; the effective sample is closer to the number of firms than to the number of firm-years.

By the design's rule (a significant year before the final year, conditional logit) H4 is met for: no layer. Appendix 3.B — Glossary of terms, statistics and notation

Term Meaning in this study
Related party transaction (RPT) A transaction between the reporting firm and a party that controls it, is controlled by it, or is one of its directors, officers, principal shareholders or their close family or affiliated entities (ASC 850; Regulation S-K Item 404).
AAER SEC Accounting and Auditing Enforcement Release: the SEC's publication of an enforcement action involving accounting or auditing matters.
Litigation release (LR) The SEC's publication of a civil court action; many RPT fraud cases appear here without an AAER.
Case firm / matched firm A firm named in an RPT-related enforcement action / a firm from the same industry and size band with no enforcement, selected by rule D4.
Matched set (cluster) One case firm and its matched firms; the unit of resampling and of cross-validation.
Firm-year One annual report of one firm; the unit of analysis.
Event time Fiscal year minus the revelation year; the window is event time −5 to −1.
Revelation date First public event revealing the problem (8-K non-reliance, investigation, auditor resignation, short report, restatement or SEC action).
Primary filing The first original annual report filed for a firm-year (amendments and duplicates are excluded).
Compustat fiscal-year rule A fiscal year ending in January–May is assigned to the previous calendar year.
CIK The SEC's Central Index Key, the firm identifier used throughout.
SIC Standard Industrial Classification code (four digits); matching falls back to three and two digits.
XBRL company facts Machine-readable financial statement values filed with the SEC (FY2009+); values are taken as first reported.
XBRL frames Cross-sectional XBRL values (here total assets) for all registrants in a period; used for the matching universe.
Item 1 / 9A / 13 Sections of Form 10-K: Business; Controls and Procedures (internal control over financial reporting); Certain Relationships and Related Transactions.
DEF 14A The definitive proxy statement, which contains board, committee and related-person disclosures.
8-K Item 4.01 / 4.02 Current-report items for a change in the certifying accountant and for non-reliance on previously issued financial statements (restatement).
ICFR / material weakness Internal control over financial reporting; a material weakness is a deficiency that creates a reasonable possibility of a material misstatement.
Big-4 Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC.
Going-concern paragraph Audit-report language raising substantial doubt about the firm's ability to continue as a going concern.
Vagueness ratio (V) Share of words from the uncertainty and weak-modal word lists in the related-party text.
Agency masking (A) Share of clauses whose agent is removed or backgrounded (agentless passive, nominalisation, existential), from a systemic-functional-linguistics analyser.
Fog index (F) Gunning Fog readability: 0.4 × (average sentence length + 100 × share of words with three or more syllables).
Relabeling / indeterminate / transparency ratios Lexicon measures of euphemistic terms, indeterminate quantifiers and fully specified sentences in the related-party text.
Drift (ΔV, ΔF) Year-on-year change of a disclosure measure within the firm.
Boilerplate similarity TF-IDF cosine similarity of the firm's related-party text with its own text a year earlier.
Specificity gap V(related-party text) − V(Item 1) of the same filing.
Peer-relative z-score (x_i − mean of the other firms in the cluster-year) ÷ their standard deviation, leave-one-out, label-free.
Peer text distance 1 − cosine similarity between the firm's TF-IDF vector and the centroid of its cluster-year peers' vectors.
TF-IDF Term frequency × inverse document frequency weighting of words and word pairs.
Text risk (stacked) Out-of-fold probability from a TF-IDF logistic regression on the related-party text, fitted in nested grouped folds with a vocabulary limited to terms used by ≥ 3 firms.
Rank-average score Each characteristic ranked to [0, 1] over all firm-years; layer score = mean of ranks; AI_SCORE = 10 × mean of layer scores (design document, steps 3–5).
TRAD_INDEX Count of the four traditional signals (material weakness, going concern, auditor change, restatement), 0–4.
Logistic regression (logit) Pr(y = 1) = 1 / (1 + exp(−x'β)); coefficients are log-odds; exp(β) is an odds ratio.
Conditional (fixed-effects) logit Logit with a separate intercept per matched set, estimated by conditioning on the number of positives in each set so the intercepts drop out; only sets with both outcomes contribute.
Cluster-robust (sandwich) standard errors Standard errors that allow arbitrary correlation of observations within a firm.
Winsorising Replacing values beyond the 1st/99th percentile with those percentiles.
Standardised coefficient / odds ratio per SD Effect of a one-standard-deviation increase of the predictor.
Holm adjustment Step-down correction for multiple tests: the smallest p is compared with α/m, the next with α/(m−1), and so on.
Mann–Whitney U test Non-parametric test of whether one group's values tend to exceed the other's; one-sided here.
Wilcoxon signed-rank test Non-parametric paired test, used for within-filing differences (RPT text versus Item 1).
Fisher's exact test Exact test of association in a 2×2 table; one-sided here.
Random forest Average of many decision trees fitted on bootstrap samples with random feature subsets.
XGBoost Gradient-boosted decision trees fitted stage-wise to the gradient of the logistic loss.
Ensemble (alert score) Mean of the three models' predicted probabilities.
Leave-one-cluster-out (LOGO) Cross-validation that holds out one matched set at a time.
Nested folds Inner cross-validation inside each training fold, used so the stacked text score never comes from a model that saw the row it scores.
Out-of-fold (OOF) Scores and SHAP values computed for rows by models that did not see them.
AUC Area under the ROC curve = probability that a random positive scores above a random negative.
PR-AUC Area under the precision–recall curve.
Cluster bootstrap Resampling matched sets (or firms) with replacement to obtain percentile confidence intervals.
Threshold at 5 % FPR The score above which at most 5 % of matched firm-years fall; fixed on out-of-fold scores.
Lead time Months from the end of the first flagged fiscal year to the revelation date.
SHAP value Shapley-value decomposition of a prediction into additive feature contributions.
Attribution stability Spearman rank correlation of feature-importance rankings across models and across bootstrap resamples.
Traceability Share of top-3 attributions that resolve to a located filing passage, signature, 8-K, XBRL tag or manifest entry.
Mediation (a, b, indirect) a = effect of x on the mediator; b = effect of the mediator on the outcome given x; indirect = a × b; c′ = direct effect.
M1 / M2 / M3 Measurement checks: coverage; reliability against human coding on a 10 % sample; distinctness of the layer scores.
A1 / A2 / A3 Attribution checks: traceability; stability; reading of the dominant layer against theory.
Core / disclosure-only stratum Cases where the RPT is the mechanism of misstatement or misappropriation / cases charged only for a disclosure failure.
External validation set Ten three-report cases and their matched firms, never used in fitting.
