Do related-party transactions give fraud away before the SEC does?

31 US firms later charged over a related-party transaction, each matched with firms from the same industry and size that were never charged. Five fiscal years before the problem became public, read from 2664 annual reports and proxy statements. Every number below can be traced to a sentence in a filing.

Each dot is one firm-year's AI risk score (the design's rank-averaged score of eight related-party characteristics). The threshold is the 95th percentile of the matched firm-years. The two distributions overlap almost entirely: the score alone does not separate the groups.

What the tests found

H1 financial layer… H2 disclosure layer… H3 governance layer… H4 earlier years… H5 adds to audit signals…

"Supported" means the predicted direction with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 using the firm-clustered standard errors the design asks for. Governance separates the groups with model-based errors only; the disclosure layer runs the opposite way; the combined score adds nothing to the audit and internal-control signals.
